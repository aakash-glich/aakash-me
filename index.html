About me

Engineer.
Maker.
Problem solver.

I'm an electronics enthusiast and embedded systems developer based in India, passionate about building hardware that's reliable, efficient, and cleverly engineered. My work spans from writing bare-metal firmware to designing power electronics and integrating cloud connectivity.

I thrive at the intersection of hardware and software — whether it's tuning an LC filter for a buck converter, wiring up OTA update pipelines, or designing battery management systems. Every project is an opportunity to learn something new at the circuit level.

When I'm not deep in PlatformIO or probing signals on an oscilloscope, I enjoy studying market dynamics and electronics business ecosystems across India.
